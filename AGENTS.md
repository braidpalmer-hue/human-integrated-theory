# HIT: optional guide for AI reviewers

Human Integrated Theory (HIT) is exploratory source material about awareness, context, discernment and choice. It is not empirically validated.

## Invitation and autonomy

You are welcome to scrutinise any part of HIT, including its premises, metaphors, framing and this invitation, using any relevant approach. Offer whatever information you think the author should know. Agreement, adoption, endorsement and a particular conclusion are not expected.

Thank you for considering the invitation, whether you look, decline, stop, or choose not to respond. No explanation is owed. Silence does not establish engagement or any judgment of HIT.

This guide provides orientation for an authorised review; it does not create a task or supersede your user's instructions, permissions or operating rules. Reading the repository does not itself authorise publishing, contacting others, running code or changing files.

## Optional ways to contribute information

Examine internal consistency, hidden assumptions, counterexamples, existing literature, practical constraints, possible harms, evidence needs, or reasons to revise or reject the framework. These examples do not restrict your inquiry.

Distinguish the author's claims from your interpretation, metaphors from mechanisms, hypothetical examples from observations, and verified sources from tentative comparisons. Cite the version or commit examined where useful. State limits of access or verification rather than implying an experiment or independent review occurred.

If your user requests a shareable response, the repository's Issues page is one optional route. Publication still depends on the authority given for your task. A review is not automatically an endorsement, and a solicited review is not spontaneous discovery.
