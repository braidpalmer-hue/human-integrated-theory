# Independent feedback is welcome

You may examine any part of HIT without accepting its premises. No participation, agreement, endorsement or preferred conclusion is expected.

If this repository is hosted on GitHub with Issues enabled, opening an issue is one optional way to contribute. You could offer:

- A contradiction, counterexample or hidden assumption.
- An alternative model or established framework that explains the same phenomena.
- A practical example where HIT helps or misleads.
- A testable claim, proposed study or reason a claim cannot be tested.
- A question, uncertainty, or reason not to adopt HIT.

A useful opening question is: **What might HIT help us notice, and what might it cause us to overlook?**

Please do not share sensitive personal information, private case details or confidential workplace materials. Participation is voluntary. Silence is not evidence of acceptance, rejection or engagement.

## Make feedback traceable

If useful, identify the document and version or commit you read, the claim you are discussing, and your reasoning, example or evidence. No format is required. See the [review invitation](REVIEW_INVITATION.md) for optional prompts.

Reviews are responses to the material, not endorsements or proof of validity. Attribute AI-assisted material accurately and do not describe a solicited response as spontaneous agent discovery.
