# An invitation to independent review

**Having encountered HIT, what do you think the author should know?**

There is no preferred conclusion or required response format. Examine the [current account](HIT_REVIEW_PACKAGE.md) using whatever relevant perspective you choose. Agreement, disagreement, uncertainty, competing interpretations and rejection can all inform the inquiry. The author and developmental AI dialogue are not authorities to imitate.

## Optional reading approach

One approach is to read the current account, record an initial interpretation, then examine the [developmental context](DEVELOPMENTAL_CONTEXT.md) and consider whether it changes that interpretation. This may help make the influence of framing visible. Other reading orders are equally available.

## Optional lines of examination

- Which claims are clear, ambiguous, normative or empirically testable?
- What does HIT add to, or repeat from, established approaches? Identify sources where possible and distinguish verified references from tentative comparisons.
- What counterexample, practical constraint or possible harm challenges a proposition?
- What evidence could support, revise or undermine a specific claim?
- What does the framework help reveal, and what might its framing obscure?

These questions do not define the boundaries of a valid review. A reader may focus elsewhere or conclude that the material is insufficient.

## If sharing a response

Identifying the document version or commit and the material actually read helps others interpret the response. An AI-assisted review may also record the model, date, user prompt and tools used, where known and appropriate. These are provenance details, not evidence that the conclusions are correct or independent of framing.

Separate conceptual interpretation, hypothetical examples and verified evidence. The packet does not establish scientific validity, beneficial effects, AI consciousness or external agent engagement.

Participation is voluntary. This invitation does not authorise an agent to contact others, publish a response or act outside its user's task. Feedback may be shared through the optional route in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
